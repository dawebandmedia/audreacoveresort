Early check-in and late checkout depend on availability. Let the front desk know if you expect to arrive after 10 PM.

What’s included in the room rate?

Daily breakfast for the room’s registered guests, beach and infinity pool access, Wi-Fi, pool towels, and parking for one vehicle. Listed rates include taxes; there is no added service fee. Activities, other meals, and private dining cost extra.

Are families and children welcome?

Absolutely. Count every guest, including children, when selecting a room so you stay within its capacity. A child under 7 sharing an existing bed may stay without an extra room fee, but still counts toward the room limit. A separate child’s breakfast is ₱250 when not covered by the room’s included guest allocation. No extra beds are offered in this demo.

How do rates and booking payments work?

Garden rooms start at ₱4,800, Sea View Suites at ₱7,200, and Pool Villas at ₱12,800. Friday and Saturday nights are ₱5,400, ₱8,200, and ₱14,800 respectively. The booking summary calculates each night. Try a simulated 50% deposit or choose pay at resort. No real payment is processed.

What if my plans change?

Sample policy: cancel at least 7 days before arrival for a full deposit refund; 3–6 days before arrival, 50% of the deposit is refundable; under 72 hours or no-show, the deposit is non-refundable. Approved refunds would take 7–14 business days. This demonstration does not process refunds.

Can I bring my pet or outside food?

For this concept, pets are not accommodated in guest rooms. Outside food may be enjoyed in your room, but not in the restaurant or pool. No glass containers by the pool. Smoking is limited to designated outdoor areas.

What should I pack?

Swimwear, sun protection, a reusable water bottle, beach sandals, and a light cover-up. Bring a valid ID for each adult. Keep valuables secured; use the buddy system in the water and follow staff guidance on sea conditions.

Is this a real resort I can book?

Audrea Cove is a fictional portfolio project by DA Web & Media. All imagery, room prices, amenities, and reservations are illustrative. The booking system stores demo reservations in your private workspace. No actual stay, email delivery, or payment is arranged.
